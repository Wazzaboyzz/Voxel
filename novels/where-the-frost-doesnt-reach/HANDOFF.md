## Standing rules for this project (apply to every future chapter)
- Target 2,000–2,500 words per chapter.
- **No em dashes anywhere in chapter prose.** Zia has stated this explicitly and repeatedly — check before presenting any chapter as done.
- **Do not change the established writing style.** Match Mara's voice (dry, controlled, engineering/structural metaphors) and Caleb's voice (steady, plainspoken, dry humor that surfaces sideways) exactly as established in chapters already written — do not drift toward a different narrative voice.
- No signs of AI/Claude authorship in the prose itself.
- EXISTING chapters: adapt from the source material, preserve established dialogue/plot beats, may add sensory/interior texture to reach word count, but don't alter established facts.
- NEW chapters: must not contradict any already-written EXISTING chapter (before or after it in sequence). Check architecture.md and adjacent chapters first.
- Checkpoint chapters (do not shift their position in the 45-chapter sequence): Ch.30 (first kiss — DONE), Ch.34 (rupture), Ch.41 (mill climax), Ch.43 (resolution).
- All filenames in `chapters/` must be lowercase (`chapter_NN.md`) — this has been enforced once already (ch.20–27 were uploaded capitalized and renamed).
- Zia is a non-technical, voice-dictation user (expect transcription garbles of names — e.g. "Noah"=Nova, "Waza boys"=Wazzaboyzz, "Vaza"=Voxel, "Watson"=Voxel).
